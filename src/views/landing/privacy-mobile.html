AI Learning Isolation

Zero Cross-Pollination

Models fine-tuned on your merchant data are strictly containerized. Weights adjusted via your proprietary datasets are never utilized to infer insights for other network participants. The synaptic pathways generated by your data belong exclusively to your instance.

Ephemeral Processing

Live inference requests are processed in volatile memory. Once an AI response is generated and transmitted, the input context is wiped from our processing clusters within milliseconds. We do not maintain logs of user prompts or generative outputs unless explicitly configured in your retention policy.
